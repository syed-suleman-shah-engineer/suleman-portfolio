About Me

“I believe the best way to learn is by interacting.”

Based in Hyderabad, Pakistan, I am a Unity Engineer and XR Developer. My work bridges competitive gaming, immersive simulations, and educational technology — creating experiences that people don't just watch, they participate in.

Whether it's a multiplayer game that sharpens reflexes, a VR simulation that prepares soldiers for the field, or a history module that transports students to ancient Egypt — interaction is the core of everything I build.

Alongside development, I teach Unity and game development with GameStorm Studio and Nexkill. Teaching forces me to deconstruct complex problems into fundamentals, which directly influences my engineering process. Every student question is a new perspective.

Currently, I am focused on scaling WitShells Studio — bringing real-time multiplayer and XR solutions to clients across the globe.
